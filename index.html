Frequently Asked Questions

How do I log my workout sets?

Tap on a set row, enter weight and reps (or distance/duration for cardio), then hit the ✓ to mark it complete. Auto-rest will start if enabled.

Can I customize my rest timers?

Yes—open the rest timer menu during a workout to adjust duration, add or subtract seconds, or skip rest altogether.

How do I switch between lbs/kg or in/cm?

During onboarding (or in your profile), select your preferred units and any existing entries will auto‐convert.

Is my data backed up online?

All data is stored locally on your device. Deleting the app removes everything—future cloud backups will be optional and consent-based.

Can I review or edit past workouts?

Yes—open the Profile screen, select any saved workout, and you can view details or adjust logged sets as needed.
