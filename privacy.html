Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 03, 2025

1. Introduction

Yoked (“we”, “our” or “us”) is committed to protecting your privacy. This Policy explains how we handle any information you enter into or generate within the Yoked app.

2. What Data We Collect

• All data—including workouts, logs, and settings—is stored locally on your device.
• We do not collect, transmit, or store any personal data on our servers at this time.

3. How We Use Your Data

• Data remains on your device and is only used to power features of the Yoked app.
• No third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services are used.

4. Data Retention & Deletion

• Your data persists on your device until you choose to delete the app.
• Uninstalling the app completely removes all stored data.

5. Future Features

As we add optional cloud backup or sync features, we will update this Policy to explain any new data collection or processing steps.

6. Children’s Privacy

Yoked is not intended for children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from minors.

7. Contact Us

If you have questions or concerns, please email us at privacy@yokedapp.com.

Data Use Policy

We do not share, sell, or rent your data. All app data stays on your device. Should we introduce any cloud-based features in the future, we will request your explicit consent before uploading anything off-device.
